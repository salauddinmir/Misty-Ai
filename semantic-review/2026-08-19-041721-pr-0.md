This final pass rechecks the Phase 38 diff after fixes for the three prior causal blockers, assessment isolation, and durable semantic/relation state. Ranked and composed WHAT answers now execute the PLAN-selected action, normalized WHAT retrieval carries the exact consumed evidence into grounding, and protected creator identity wins over conflicting learned facts. ## High-level view

RECALL normalizes WHAT targets before evidence collection, REASON ranks individual answers and multi-fact compositions, PLAN carries the selected candidate, and ACT treats a supported selection as authoritative. This closes the previous split where legacy WHAT handling could choose a different answer or lose provenance.

Protected self-identity is represented as `protected_self_model` evidence with a higher trust priority than semantic or graph facts. The trust decision remains visible in the REASON → PLAN → ACT path rather than being hidden in a late response shortcut.

Assessment clones enter an explicit no-learning mode, remove copied persistence hooks, and skip procedural reinforcement, Hebbian updates, long-term learning, and consolidation. Pre/post comparison uses isolated clones over frozen cases and applies only the committed delta to the post-learning clone.

Semantic facts restore with source and confidence, and mutable relation weights update by durable relation ID. **Likely, deferred:** full episode hydration and graph-wide decay/persistence remain scaling work rather than release blockers at the current verified size.
